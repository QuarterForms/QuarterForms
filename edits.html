Questions

Before you ask

Do you add music?

No. I do not add music to edits. You add your own track to the finished video yourself. I do the cuts, transitions, sound effects, mixing and subtitles.

Can you make my thumbnail too?

Yes. A custom thumbnail is an add-on for an extra fee of $10 per video, on any package.

What platforms do you edit for?

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or wherever you post. Tell me the platform and I will cut the video to fit it, vertical or horizontal.

What kind of videos do you edit best?

Gaming. I have experience with gaming videos, highlights and funny-moment compilations, and with commentary. I can edit other types of video too, so ask if you are not sure.

How do I send you my footage?

Upload it to Google Drive, Dropbox or any similar service and send me the download link. Check that the link is open so I can download the files.

How do revisions work?

A revision round is one set of notes on the current edit. Starter includes 1 round, Standard 2 and Creator Style 3.

How do I pay?

50% when we agree on scope, 50% when you approve the final edit. The final video is sent once the second payment clears.
