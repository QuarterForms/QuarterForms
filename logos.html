About

Hi, I'm a freelance designer who makes logos for people starting something new.

A good logo is simple enough to remember and sturdy enough to use everywhere: on a shop sign, a phone screen, a stamp, a sticker. That is how I design them, and I test every mark in those conditions before I send it.

I work with one client at a time and reply to messages myself. If you are not sure what you need, tell me about your business and I will suggest where to start.

The checks every logo passes

Before delivery, each logo is tested against these.

  • Readable at 16 pixels
  • Works reversed on dark
  • Holds up in one colour
  • Still clear in greyscale
  • Fits a round profile photo
Questions

Before you ask

Who owns the logo?

You do. When the final payment arrives, all rights to the finished logo transfer to you. I keep the right to show it in my portfolio unless you ask me not to.

What do you need from me to get started?

A few sentences about your business, who it is for, and two or three logos you like (and why). If you have colours or a name you already love, send those too.

How do revisions work?

A revision round is one set of feedback on the current design. Starter includes 2 rounds, Standard 4 and Identity 4. Feedback that changes the whole direction after sketches are approved counts as a new concept.

Can you redesign my existing logo?

Yes. Send the current file and tell me what is not working. Redesigns follow the same packages and prices as new logos.

How do I pay?

50% when we agree on scope, 50% when you approve the final design. Final files are sent once the second payment clears.

What if I do not like any of the concepts?

Tell me what is missing. I will rework the closest concept using your notes as part of your revision rounds.
